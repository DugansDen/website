Meet the Den

A Roaming Card Shop, Built on Trust.

Dugan's Legendary Den is a US-based Pokémon TCG business based in Austin, Texas. We operate primarily at card shows in Austin and the DFW area, with plans to expand into other regions.

Our setup is built around accessibility, variety, and real-time market movement — so we can serve both collectors and competitive buyers with strong inventory turnover and consistent demand at live events.
